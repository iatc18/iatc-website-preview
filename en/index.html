METHOD

AI Kaizen: improve daily, measure weekly

We don’t teach tools to finish a syllabus. Every 10–15 minute lesson is tied to a real task, results are submitted daily, and the strongest learners become AI Mentors for their colleagues. Our philosophy: train people to become better than their trainers.
