Payment & Deferral Policy

Clear terms on money build trust. This page explains how to pay, how invoices are issued, and how to defer, change or transfer your place.

  • Listed prices include VAT. There are no hidden fees.
  • IATC accepts bank transfer and online payment only. IATC does not accept cash.
  • Pay only into an account in IATC's company name. IATC will never ask you to pay into a personal account.
  • Every payment comes with an e-invoice sent by email.
  • 3+3 evening course: one free deferral, for up to 6 months, into a later intake with updated content.

Scope

This policy applies to all tuition and service fees paid to IATC:

  • 3+3 evening course (3 in-person sessions at our centre + 3 weeks of online practice coaching), whether you enrol yourself or your company sponsors you.
  • Services for businesses: on-site AI diagnostic, 1+5 in-house course, 3-month AI Coaching. For these services, the terms of the contract or quotation agreed by both parties take precedence; this policy applies to anything the contract does not cover.

Fees published on the website or in a quotation include value-added tax (VAT). The applicable fee is the one in effect when you enrol and pay.

Payment methods

IATC accepts only the two methods below and does not accept cash.

Bank transfer or VietQR

  • Transfer to the account in IATC's company name. The account number and VietQR code are given in your enrolment confirmation email, quotation or contract.
  • Payment reference: Full name – Class code – Phone number (for example: NGUYEN VAN A – 33K01 – 0901234567).

Online payment

Through the payment gateway on iatcvn.com, once online payment is enabled on the enrolment page. The gateway's name is shown clearly at the payment step.

Avoid scams. IATC only receives payment into the account in IATC's company name. IATC will never ask you to pay into a personal account, does not accept cash, and never asks for passwords or OTP codes. If in doubt, call +84 38 997 3818 before transferring. IATC is not responsible for money paid to any other individual or organisation.

Enrolment and seat confirmation (3+3 course)

  1. Reserve a seat through the website form, hotline or Zalo.
  2. IATC confirms seat availability and sends payment details within 1 working day.
  3. You pay within 3 working days. After that, the seat may be offered to the next applicant.
  4. IATC sends your enrolment confirmation and receipt by email or Zalo within 1 working day of receiving payment.

Your seat is only guaranteed once payment is received. When a class is nearly full, seats go in order of payment.

Invoices

  • IATC issues an e-invoice for every payment and sends it by email.
  • Please provide your invoice details (individual or company name, tax code, address, invoice email) when you enrol, and no later than payment.
  • If an invoice was issued with the details you provided, any adjustment or replacement follows Vietnam's e-invoice regulations.

Refunds for the 3+3 course

Timing is based on when IATC receives your written request (email or Zalo message).

Deferral, session change, transfer (3+3 course)

  • Deferral: free, once, for up to 6 months from the start date of your original class. You join a later intake with updated content. If fees have gone up, you pay nothing extra.
  • Missing a session for a valid reason (illness, business travel, family matters): make it up free of charge in another class within 6 months.
  • Changing schedule (Mon-Wed-Fri evenings to Tue-Thu-Sat or vice versa) before the start date: free, if seats are available.
  • Transferring your place to someone else: free, with at least 1 working day's notice before the first session, including the new participant's name and contact details.
  • If the deferral period expires unused, the fees are not refundable.

Services for businesses

Payment

The payment schedule is set out in the contract or quotation. Typically: for the 1+5 in-house course, 50% on signing and 50% after the on-site training day; for 3-month AI Coaching, monthly payments at the start of each month.

On-site AI diagnostic

The diagnostic fee is 100% credited against an in-house or Coaching contract signed within 60 days of the diagnostic.

Substituting participants

Companies may substitute participants free of charge at any time before the training date.

If IATC changes the schedule

  • A 3+3 class does not reach the minimum size, or IATC must reschedule it: IATC gives at least 3 days' notice before the start date. You choose a 100% refund, a transfer to another class or a deferral.
  • Business services: IATC gives early notice and agrees a new date with the company at no extra cost. If the company does not accept the new date, IATC refunds 100% of the amount paid for services not yet delivered.
  • Force majeure (natural disasters, epidemics, decisions of state authorities): both parties reschedule without penalty. If the service cannot be delivered, IATC refunds the undelivered portion.

How to make a request

  1. Email hcm@iatcvn.com with the subject "Deferral/schedule change request – Full name – Class code", or message us on Zalo, attaching your payment receipt.
  2. IATC replies within 2 working days.
  3. Once both parties confirm, IATC pays the refund within 7 working days by transfer to the account the payment came from. To receive it in a different account, the payer must confirm in writing.
  4. If you paid through a payment gateway or by card, the refund follows the bank's or gateway's process and may take longer.

Refunds on invoiced amounts are handled through an invoice adjustment as required by law. Companies are asked to sign an adjustment record when needed.

Offers and gifts

  • For discounted fees (early bird, group, referral), refunds are calculated on the amount actually paid.
  • Gifts, vouchers and complimentary materials cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Offers cannot be combined unless the programme states otherwise.

Complaints

Contact hcm@iatcvn.com or call +84 38 997 3818. IATC acknowledges your complaint within 2 working days and gives its answer within 7 working days. Both parties will first try to resolve the matter by negotiation. If no agreement is reached, you may seek support from the state authority for consumer protection, or take the matter to court under Vietnamese law.

Validity and changes

This policy applies to enrolments and contracts made on or after the update date shown at the top of this page. When IATC changes the policy, the new version does not apply retroactively to people who have already paid, unless the new version is more favourable to the learner.
