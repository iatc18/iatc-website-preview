Scope and legal basis

This policy explains how IATC collects, uses, stores and protects personal data when you visit iatcvn.com, submit a form, contact us by email, phone or Zalo, or take part in an IATC class, workshop, training or coaching programme.

It follows Vietnam's Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15, Decree No. 356/2025/ND-CP guiding that Law, the Law on Protection of Consumers' Rights No. 19/2023/QH15 and the Law on E-commerce No. 122/2025/QH15. It does not cover third-party websites linked from iatcvn.com.

Consent and withdrawal

  • You give consent through a clear action: ticking a box on a form, replying by email or signing an enrolment form. Consent boxes on our website are never pre-ticked.
  • Agreeing to receive newsletters is a separate choice. If you decline, you can still receive advice and attend classes as normal.
  • Class photos and video: IATC only publishes images in which you can be identified if you agree. You can ask us to remove them at any time.
  • Withdrawing consent: email hcm@iatcvn.com, or click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any newsletter. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out. IATC will still keep data it must retain under a contract or the law (such as invoices).

Who we share data with

IATC shares data only in the cases below, and only the minimum needed:

  • The company that sponsors your training (in-house courses, coaching): IATC reports attendance, scores, assignment progress and SOP progress to that company. This is part of the training contract between IATC and the company.
  • Service providers acting for IATC: Google (email, the spreadsheet that stores website enquiries), GitHub (website hosting), Cloudflare (domain management, network security), Zalo (when you message us there), banks, payment gateways and e-invoice providers. They process data only on IATC's instructions and under their own security commitments.
  • Organisations that co-host a class or provide the venue (if any): only the list needed for attendance and room arrangements. They are named in the course opening notice.
  • Competent state authorities when they make a request under the law.

If IATC is reorganised, merged or transfers its business, data will only be transferred if it remains protected at least as well as under this policy. IATC will notify you before any such transfer.

Storage outside Vietnam

Some of the providers listed above (Google, GitHub, Cloudflare) store and process data on servers outside Vietnam. Under Article 20 of the Law on Personal Data Protection, this is a cross-border transfer of personal data. IATC only uses providers with internationally recognised security commitments, limits the data transferred to what is necessary, and completes any filings with the competent authority when the law requires.

When the period ends, IATC deletes, destroys or de-identifies the data (turning it into aggregate figures that cannot identify anyone).

How we protect your data

  • The whole website runs over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Admin accounts use two-factor sign-in. Only people who need data for their work can see it, and access is removed when their role ends.
  • IATC staff, trainers, AI Mentors and collaborators are bound by confidentiality commitments regarding learner information.
  • IATC does not keep learner lists on personal devices or unencrypted USB drives.
  • If a data breach occurs, IATC acts immediately to contain it and notifies the specialised personal data protection authority and affected people within the time limits set by law.

No system is completely secure. You can help by keeping your passwords private and telling IATC straight away if you notice anything unusual.

Your rights and obligations

Under Article 4 of the Law on Personal Data Protection, you have the right to:

  1. Be informed about how IATC processes your personal data.
  2. Give or refuse consent, and withdraw consent.
  3. Access and correct your data, or ask for it to be corrected.
  4. Request a copy of your data, its deletion or restricted processing; object to processing.
  5. Complain, report, sue and claim compensation in accordance with the law.
  6. Ask IATC or the competent authority to take measures to protect your personal data.

You are responsible for providing complete and accurate information and for respecting other people's personal data.

When doing AI exercises. Do not enter colleagues' or customers' personal data, or trade secrets, into AI tools without permission. IATC trainers will show you how to clean and anonymise data before using AI.

Cookies and analytics

iatcvn.com currently uses no advertising cookies or behavioural tracking tools (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel). The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers. Our network security service may set technical cookies that are strictly necessary to block attacks.

If IATC adds analytics or advertising tools, it will update this policy and ask for your consent before enabling any non-essential tools.

Children's data

IATC's services are designed for working professionals and businesses. IATC does not knowingly collect data from children (under 16). Where processing is unavoidable, IATC only proceeds with the consent of a parent or guardian as required by law. If we find we have received a child's data without such consent, we will delete it.
